terrella

An interactive globe for the web. Highlight countries and regions, place markers, and pick the look. Drag to spin, flick to throw, hover a marker for its local time.

A terrella is a small model of the Earth. William Gilbert built one in 1600 to study magnetism, which is roughly what this is: a small model of the world you can hold and turn.
